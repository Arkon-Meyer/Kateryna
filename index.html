Behind the Work

Every painting takes 40 to 60 hours of hand-crafted brushwork in Corel Painter. No shortcuts, no AI — every stroke is intentional.

Drag to reveal the sketch beneath

It starts with pencil on paper

Before any pixel is placed, the composition lives as pencil sketches — quick, rough, exploring how the figures sit in the frame and where the light falls. This is where the emotional tone is decided, long before colour enters the picture.

Then comes the slow work

The sketch is rebuilt digitally in Corel Painter — layer by layer, brushstroke by brushstroke. Values first, establishing depth and drama in monochrome. Then colour is introduced gradually, each hue chosen to serve the story the painting tells. Details emerge over days: textures, reflections, strands of hair, the glow of light on skin.

This is work that can't be rushed and can't be generated. It's the difference between a painting and a prompt.

About

Katarina Sokolova

Digital illustrator & Corel Painter Master

I'm a CG artist who has worked as a freelance illustrator since 2000. My style blends realism, fantasy, sensuality, and storytelling — placing characters and objects in surreal worlds of dreams and imagination.

I create digital illustrations for international editorials, advertising agencies, publishing houses, and magazines across five continents. My work has been featured on book covers, puzzles, calendars, and in the world's leading digital art publications.

In 2016, I was honoured with the Corel Painter Master Award. My artwork was selected for the cover and advertising campaign of Corel Painter XI, and I've served as an official beta tester for the software since 2007.
